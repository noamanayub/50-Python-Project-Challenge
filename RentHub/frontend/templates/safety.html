Safety Guidelines

Keeping our community safe

Renter Safety

  • Always verify item condition before accepting
  • Take photos before and after rental period
  • Report any issues within 24 hours
  • Follow item-specific usage instructions
  • Return items on time and in good condition

Owner Safety

  • Verify renter identity and ratings
  • Set clear terms and conditions
  • Document item condition with photos
  • Use secure payment methods only
  • Report suspicious activity immediately
